      Memory, concurrency and saved sessions
      Go heap is sampled at cell boundaries; it is not total process RAM. Goroutines and native handles can survive pauses. Synchronize background work explicitly. Stop, timeout or a process crash discards live values. Export datasets and models before stopping. Saved experiments keep source, outputs and command history; replay executes that history again.
      All cells compile as one Go function. Defer runs at function exit, not at cell completion. Repeating a cell can accumulate defers and goroutines. Stop forcibly terminates the worker.
